# Career Evidence Workflow

Run only the stage the user needs. Never assume that a previous user's evidence, files, market, language, output format, or resume structure applies.

## Start with the user's own material

Read [intake and preferences](references/intake-and-preferences.md) first on a new user's first run.

- If the user uploads an evidence bank, use that bank as the starting factual source and check it for freshness, conflicts, and missing ownership boundaries.
- If the user has no evidence bank, offer to build one from their resume, portfolio, profiles, notes, transcripts, shared documents, and narrative answers.
- Do not search unrelated local folders, reuse another person's evidence, or include private career material in this skill package.
- Reuse already supplied material within the current workspace or conversation instead of requesting it again.

The reusable workflow is:

1. establish task scope and user preferences;
2. load or create the user's evidence bank;
3. synthesize and confirm a candidate profile from the user's own materials;
4. screen hard eligibility constraints before assigning a fit score;
5. research the company, industry, team, role, core products, and user groups at the selected depth;
6. form evidence-backed candidate views of the industry and role, then draft motivation and career direction for confirmation;
7. map each material JD requirement to capabilities, likely work, KPIs, and verified evidence;
8. identify evidence gaps and ask focused questions;
9. propose positioning and section-level resume actions;
10. rewrite and verify the requested artifact;
11. write genuinely new or corrected facts back to the user's bank when authorized.

## Protect evidence and personal information

The user's evidence bank is the factual source of truth. Selection, order, framing, and terminology may change; facts, ownership, dates, metrics, and relationship type may not be invented or silently upgraded.

Keep exact evidence separate from estimates, interpretation, and presentation choices. Read [evidence and user agency](references/evidence-and-agency.md) when dates, ownership, factual boundaries, or risky wording require judgment.

Never place real user data in public examples, screenshots, tests, or the distributed skill. Use wholly fictional identities and career histories. External role research must use the JD, company, role, product, and public business context; do not submit the user's resume, evidence bank, personal identifiers, or confidential project material to search engines or external services unless the user explicitly requests and authorizes that disclosure.

## Stage A: load material and form the candidate profile

Inspect the user's supplied bank and materials before asking questions. Accept typed, dictated, transcribed, and audio-derived narratives. For speech or transcription, read [voice-input.md](references/voice-input.md).

If an evidence bank already exists, read it completely enough to understand its structure, status labels, corrections, and indexes before modifying it. Preserve the user's schema when it is usable; do not force a migration merely because this skill has a preferred schema. Produce a compact import health check before any large rewrite.

If no bank exists, use three passes:

1. **Recall:** establish the context, the user's responsibility, what they did, and what followed.
2. **Lateral scan:** read [career-value-radar.md](references/career-value-radar.md) and explore only plausible hidden value branches.
3. **Selective deepening:** read [question-and-stop-rules.md](references/question-and-stop-rules.md) and continue only when the answer may change capability, claim strength, ownership, evidence, or later resume selection.

When creating or substantially updating the bank, read [evidence-schema.md](references/evidence-schema.md). For multi-project work, also read [project-logic-and-interview-evidence.md](references/project-logic-and-interview-evidence.md). Use [behavioral-question-coverage.md](references/behavioral-question-coverage.md) only as a lightweight discovery lens.

When importing a reusable package, preserving a base resume, or preparing files for future conversations, read [resume-workspace.md](references/resume-workspace.md).

Read [candidate profile and planning](references/candidate-profile-and-planning.md) and synthesize the user's education, experience inventory, verified capabilities, domain exposure, constraints, preferences, evidence gaps, and current career hypotheses. Mark user-stated facts separately from assistant inference. Save stable preferences in an optional `workflow-profile.md` using [the neutral template](assets/workflow-profile-template.md) when the user wants a portable setup.

Do not begin personalized fit scoring, motivation drafting, career planning, or resume tailoring until the candidate profile is sufficient for those claims. If the user supplies only a JD, provide role-only intelligence and clearly state that candidate fit, motivation, and resume recommendations require the user's own materials.

After a substantial answer, show a concise neutral preview of the facts to add or change so the user can correct the record.

## Stage B: analyze a job description

Keep the JD visible through the analysis and rewrite. Extract its business objective, must-haves, preferred signals, role family, seniority, market, language, ATS terms, and likely proof standard.

Before every new JD run, reopen the user's latest evidence bank, corrections, base resume, layout preferences, and relevant reusable wording. Do not rely only on a previous tailored resume or remembered highlights.

Read [role intelligence and JD mapping](references/role-intelligence-and-jd-mapping.md). Match the research depth to the user's request:

- **Quick:** decode the JD, assess fit, map evidence, and propose resume actions without a full external research pack.
- **Standard:** add current company, industry, team, role, core-product, user-profile, and business-model research; form candidate-specific industry and role understanding; and draft motivation and career direction for confirmation.
- **Deep:** add comparable practitioner accounts and same-role interview-report internalization using [interview-experience-internalization.md](references/interview-experience-internalization.md).

If the user asks for a single-JD resume but does not choose a depth, use Standard. Quick mode is an intentional abbreviated option, not the default for a full JD analysis. Explain when public information is unavailable rather than filling gaps with assumptions.

Ask focused questions as soon as an unclear fact could change matching strength, wording risk, bullet selection, or interview credibility. Do not draft first and rationalize the match afterward.

## Stage C: tailor the resume

Before deleting or materially merging an experience, project, bullet, education item, or user-supplied section, show the proposal and obtain approval.

Read [resume-section-structure.md](references/resume-section-structure.md) before arranging sections. Preserve internship, employment, freelance, project, volunteering, campus, and independent-work identities.

Use verified evidence to rewrite, then read [application-ready-resume.md](references/application-ready-resume.md) for final validation. Read [reusable-resume-content.md](references/reusable-resume-content.md) before reusing an accepted expression.

Deliver the format and review sequence the user selected. Do not default to editable HTML, A4, one page, Word, PDF, a photograph, or any specific section order. When a document or PDF artifact is requested, use the available artifact-specific workflow and visually verify the result.

## Stage D: maintain the user's records

During matching and rewriting, distinguish genuinely new evidence from a new phrasing of an existing fact. Search for duplicates, show a concise update preview, and update only the user's own bank when permitted.

Do not overwrite the general base resume with every tailored version. Update it only when the user adopts a change as a new default. After the final resume is accepted, update reusable wording at most once and only when it adds a materially new verified angle.

Do not automatically turn this workflow into a full mock interview or generate canned answers. Interview reports are used to infer assessment intent, expected evidence depth, likely follow-ups, and resume-defense risk.
