# Same-Role Interview Experience Research and Internalization

Read this reference at Deep analysis depth or when the user explicitly asks for comparable interview experiences.

## Purpose

Interview reports reveal capabilities interviewers actually test, expected proof depth, and follow-up questions that expose weak stories. They are directional evidence, not an official question bank or a source of answers to memorize.

Never copy another candidate's answer or claim their experience. Extract assessment logic and rebuild the evidence from the current user's own bank.

## Search strategy

Search the exact company and role first, then comparable roles in the same business line, market, seniority, and recruiting cycle. Choose sources relevant to the target market and use only publicly accessible material.

For mainland-China roles, sources may include Xiaohongshu, 牛客、知乎、脉脉, official recruiting material, and practitioner posts. For other markets, consider locally relevant professional networks, candidate communities, employer-review sites, alumni sources, and practitioner accounts. Do not bypass logins or access controls.

For every source, record:

- company, business line, role, seniority, round, and reported date when available;
- firsthand account, repost, coaching summary, comment, or inference;
- why it is comparable to the target JD;
- source link and confidence.

Summarize copyrighted posts instead of reproducing long passages.

## Internalization method

Cluster differently worded questions by assessment intent. For each family, determine:

1. what the interviewer is trying to judge;
2. what ownership, decision logic, process detail, metric definition, result, failure analysis, or business judgment would count as evidence;
3. which exact record in the user's bank is strongest;
4. likely second- and third-level probes;
5. missing facts and resulting resume-defense risk;
6. whether the resume should keep, move up, rewrite, quantify, clarify, add, or omit a claim.

Use localized headings for this semantic table:

| Question family | Assessment intent | Evidence requirement | Candidate evidence | Follow-up probes | Evidence gap | Resume action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Label candidate evidence as `Direct`, `Adjacent`, `Weak`, or `Absent`.

## Confidence boundaries

Label findings as observed question, recurring pattern, adjacent signal, inference, or unknown. Do not promise a question will appear. If comparable reports are unavailable, state that limitation and rely on the JD and verified role evidence without fabricating interview intelligence.
