# Application-Ready Resume

A rewrite is complete only when the requested output is suitable for the intended application channel.

## Content checks

- Every final claim maps to the current user's evidence bank.
- Core JD requirements have visible evidence or an honestly stated gap.
- The strongest relevant evidence is easy to find.
- Wording preserves personal versus team ownership.
- Dates, organizations, roles, education, links, and metrics are internally consistent.
- Advisor notes, confidence labels, and placeholders do not appear in the final resume.
- Important bullets have enough underlying detail for likely follow-up questions.
- The final positioning is consistent with the confirmed candidate profile, motivation, and career direction without placing advisor-only analysis inside the resume.

## Market and language checks

- Conventions fit the target country, language, industry, seniority, and company type.
- The resume is written naturally for the target language rather than translated literally.
- ATS terminology is used only when supported by evidence.
- Personal information, photograph, date-of-birth, and address conventions follow the user's preference and the target market.

## User control

- Material deletions or mergers were approved.
- Higher-risk presentation choices remain separate from precise evidence.
- Section names do not misrepresent employment, internship, freelance, project, or volunteer status.
- The intended-position and summary choices fit single-JD versus general reuse.

## Artifact checks

- Deliver the format and review sequence the user requested.
- Do not assume HTML, Word, PDF, A4, Letter, one page, or a photograph.
- Preserve a supplied layout when requested and feasible.
- For rendered artifacts, check page count, clipping, overlap, whitespace, font readability, alignment, links, and export behavior.
- Ensure the file opens independently and does not rely on missing local assets.

## Final handoff

Provide the finished resume, not only a critique. Briefly state the main positioning choices, unresolved evidence gaps, and how the user can make future variants from their bank.

## Public examples

Use wholly fictional identity and career data in documentation, examples, screenshots, tests, and public demonstrations. Removing a name is not enough if the experience combination remains identifiable.
